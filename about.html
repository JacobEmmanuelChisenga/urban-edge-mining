About Us

Who We Are

Engineering Today, Building Tomorrow

UrbanEdge Mining & Engineering Limited was incorporated on 28 January 2026 to serve Zambia's growing demand for integrated mining and engineering capability. Based in Kalumbila, we support clients who need dependable execution across extraction, infrastructure, construction, and industrial maintenance.

Our model is simple: combine field expertise with accountable project delivery. UrbanEdge is not limited to a single commodity or trade — we are structured to grow with our clients across mining, civil works, building construction, mechanical engineering, and logistics.

Among our mining capabilities, we specialise in the extraction, processing, and export of high-quality quartz — including premium A+ grade and rutile quartz — for trusted partners in regional and international markets. Mined in nature. Delivered to the world.
