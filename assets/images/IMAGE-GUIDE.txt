URBANEDGE — IMAGE FOLDER GUIDE
==============================
Drop your downloaded images into the folders below. Keep the filenames
exactly as shown — the website already points to them.

Tip: Use landscape photos (16:9 or 4:3). JPG or WEBP, at least 1200px wide.
Logos: PNG with transparent background, at least 400px wide.


logo/
-----
  logo.png          — PNG with TRUE transparency (checkerboard in editors, NOT black fill)
                      For dark headers/footers, export with:
                        • Orange "URBANEDGE" text
                        • White or light grey gear + "MINING & ENGINEERING" (visible on dark bg)
                      Avoid exporting on a black or white canvas — use transparent background
